What he owns

Design systems

Two published Figma libraries built on WCAG 2.2 from the first token rather than retrofitted — hundreds of components and tokens, replacing an Adobe XD-era system across more than twenty product files. Accessibility as a build constraint, not an audit afterwards.

Primary ink measured at 16.2:1. Minimum target height 40px, 44px for row-level actions.

AI interaction design

Nine patterns specified end to end for an enterprise AI assistant, against Microsoft's Copilot guidance, Responsible AI principles and the HAX toolkit. The work others skip is the failure surface: what the assistant does when it is slow, wrong, empty, or about to move money. Trained in workshops run with Microsoft's design team.

Data-dense interfaces

Navigators, data grids, filter systems, mass operations — and retail execution and shelf-level assortment for grocery. Rebuilt the grid standard across every module as one pattern replacing per-screen styling.

Table type 11px → 14px. Row action targets 16px → 44px.

Research and rigour

36 user interviews with commercial teams at global manufacturers and grocery retailers, and a release-by-release UX audit programme — 85 findings across three releases, each classified and linked to a spec rather than filed and forgotten.

Independent work — the part nobody asked him to do

A job watcher, and a postmortem on his own failures

Built in the open while job-hunting: a system that reads company job boards directly from their applicant tracking systems and emails only genuinely new roles. Public, MIT licensed, 54 tests passing on CI.

The part worth reading is docs/postmortem.md — a written account of the three ways his own first version failed, including the one that matters: it sent six false alerts in two days and he stopped opening it. The fix was moving every judgement out of a prompt and into deterministic code with a regression test.

Accessibility auditing

Audits against WCAG 2.2 and EN 301 549 — measured, not scanned. A first automated pass on one site returned twelve findings of which eleven were false positives; the one that survived was a contrast failure that only appears on a hovered or selected table row. Measuring rendered pairs in context, rather than declared token values, is the whole difference. He audits against the standard; a lawyer determines compliance.
